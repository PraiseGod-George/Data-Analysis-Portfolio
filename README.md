##  Key Business Insights

###  Product Performance
Technology products generated the highest profit margins.

**Insight:** Focus on high-margin products through better inventory planning and targeted promotions.


###  Sales Team Performance
A small group of sales representatives consistently outperformed others.

**Insight:** Use top performers as benchmarks for training and performance improvement.


###  Operational Costs
Certain cities recorded significantly higher COGS due to higher business activity.

**Insight:** Ensure cost increases are matched with proportional revenue growth.


### Customer Trends
Customer transaction volumes fluctuated across the reporting period.

**Insight:** Identify seasonal trends to improve planning for inventory and marketing.
